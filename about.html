Managing Director

Prasad D.S.
Managing Director

Prasad D.S. is a manufacturing professional with over 26 years of experience in production, operations, quality systems, process improvement, and manufacturing excellence. He holds a Diploma in Mechanical Engineering (DME) and a Bachelor of Engineering in Industrial Engineering from the College of Engineering, Guindy, Anna University. He began his career as a Quality Engineer in the automotive components industry and later worked with Lucas TVS as a Senior Production Engineer. His experience includes production planning, quality improvement, process optimization, cost control, plant operations, team leadership, and customer relations.

Having also served as a General Manager in a private manufacturing company, he has successfully led teams, improved productivity, optimized processes, reduced costs, and ensured timely delivery with strong quality standards. As Managing Director, he brings technical expertise, practical leadership, and a strong commitment to operational excellence, customer satisfaction, and sustainable growth.
